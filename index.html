Technology evolves at an incredible pace, continually offering new opportunities to address and overcome societal challenges. At the Interdisciplinary Methods for Applied Cyber-physical Technologies in Society research lab, we embrace this rapid advancement, developing integrated cyber-physical systems that push the boundaries of what is possible, delivering pragmatic solutions for real-world issues that impact our communities.

Our projects range from developing smart systems for cleaning and inspection in large-scale solar plants, to creating assistive technology to improve daily life for individuals with disabilities, ensuring everyone can benefit from technological advancements.

Interested in joining IMPACTLab?

We welcome motivated students interested in contributing to research. Prior research experience is not required, but curiosity, commitment, and a willingness to learn are essential.
