Want to build cyber-physical systems that tackle real societal challenges? Looking for an enriching research experience, and the chance to publish your work at academic conferences and journals? Or do you have an idea or a problem you think we could work on together?

We are always interested in hearing from curious and motivated students who want to contribute to our research.

At IMPACTLab, students have opportunities to work on research problems involving cyber-physical systems, robotics, artificial intelligence, and related technologies. You do not need prior research experience to get started. What matters most is your curiosity, commitment, reliability, and willingness to learn.

Have a look at our projects and publications to see what we're working on and what sparks your interest.

Research Opportunities for Students

If you're interested in a research role, start by reading our short guide. It explains how we approach research, what we expect from students, what you can expect from us, and how to apply.

Read First: Research Philosophy and Expectations

Research is rewarding, but it also takes time, independence, and patience, so take a moment to reflect on whether it's the right commitment for you right now. Most students start with independent study credit or as volunteers; paid positions usually go to students who have already worked with us and built a track record.

We can only supervise a limited number of students at a time, so we can't offer a spot to everyone who applies.

Applying Through SURA or QSIURP?

Great! Please check the CMU-Q Undergraduate Research Office for deadlines and the timeline, and follow the same steps described above.

Like Building Software?

We also welcome students who want to help build the software systems behind our research. Ideally you've taken 17-313 or have similar software development experience. Start with the guide above as well.
